Recovery procedure
  1. Download and store a recent snapshot away from the VPS.
  2. Stop EnAuth before restoring a database file.
  3. Preserve the current database, place the selected snapshot at your configured DB_PATH, then start EnAuth.
  4. Check the health endpoint and sign in before removing the preserved copy.
